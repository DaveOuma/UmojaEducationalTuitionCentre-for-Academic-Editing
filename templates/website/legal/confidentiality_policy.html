Confidentiality Policy

Last updated: {% now "F Y" %}

We understand that manuscripts submitted for editing are often unpublished research. We take the following measures to protect that confidentiality:

  • Documents are stored in private storage, not published or publicly accessible.
  • Access to your documents is limited to you, your assigned editor, and staff directly involved in your assignment.
  • Downloads are authenticated and access is checked against your account and assignment, not by a guessable or shared link.
  • We log document access for accountability.

No system is completely secure. We implement the measures above in good faith, and we will disclose to affected clients if we become aware of unauthorized access to their documents.
